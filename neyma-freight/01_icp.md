# Neyma Freight ICP

## Purpose

Define who Neyma Freight should research, qualify, and draft outreach for in v0.

Neyma Freight is an internal, operator-led AI SDR workflow for finding freight forwarders and customs brokers with visible manual-document pain. It does not send outreach automatically.

## Locked Target

Target companies:

- Freight forwarders
- Customs brokers
- Approx. 10-40 staff
- Operators with visible logistics-document intake or documentation-heavy workflows

Decision-maker titles:

- Founder
- Managing Director
- COO
- VP Operations

Secondary contacts only when a primary decision-maker is unavailable:

- Head of Operations
- Operations Manager
- Import Manager
- Export Manager

## Locked Pain

The prospect likely spends staff time manually copying data from logistics PDFs into a TMS, brokerage system, spreadsheet, or internal process.

Common documents:

- Commercial invoices
- Customs declarations
- Bills of lading
- Manifests
- Carrier PDFs
- Arrival notices
- Packing lists

## Public Pain Signals

Signal A: manual intake workflow.

Examples:

- "Download this PDF form, fill it out, email it back"
- Email-only quote or shipment intake process
- Static quote/contact form with no instant pricing or structured workflow
- Instructions to email shipment documents manually

Signal B: hiring for document-heavy operations work.

Relevant titles:

- Manifest Clerk
- Data Entry Clerk
- Logistics Coordinator
- Import/Export Specialist
- Documentation Clerk
- Customs Entry Writer
- Brokerage Entry Clerk

## Offer

Done-for-you automation that extracts structured line items from messy logistics documents and pushes them into the operator's workflow.

Keep the offer small and concrete:

- Extract line items from PDFs
- Reduce manual re-keying
- Start with one document type or lane
- Work with current systems instead of replacing the TMS

## Fit Rules

Qualify when:

- The company matches freight forwarding or customs brokerage.
- Headcount appears roughly 10-40 staff.
- There is at least one real Signal A or Signal B with evidence URL.
- A plausible operations decision-maker can be identified.

Disqualify when:

- The company is a carrier, warehouse-only provider, marketplace, software vendor, or enterprise 3PL outside the target size.
- No real manual-document signal can be found.
- The only evidence is generic logistics copy without a manual workflow or hiring signal.
- The company appears too large, too small, inactive, or outside the operating region being researched.

## Evidence Standard

Every pain claim must cite an evidence URL.

Every hook must be grounded in Signal A or Signal B.

Do not infer pain from industry alone. If evidence is weak, set `State` to `NEEDS_REVIEW` or `DISQUALIFIED`.
