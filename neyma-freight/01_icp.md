# Neyma Freight ICP

## Purpose

Define who Neyma Freight should research, qualify, and draft outreach for in v0.

Neyma Freight is an internal, operator-led AI SDR workflow for finding small freight brokerages with visible carrier-invoice, POD, billing, or reconciliation pain. It does not send outreach automatically.

## Locked Target

Target companies:

- Truckload freight brokerages
- Approx. 5-20 staff, with 10-40 acceptable when the operation is still lean
- Roughly 100-500 completed loads/month when volume can be inferred
- Operators using a TMS such as McLeod, Aljex, TAI, TMW, ARK, EZ Loader, or a spreadsheet-backed process
- Teams where carrier invoices, PODs, lumper receipts, billing, settlements, or carrier payables appear to move through email, fax, portals, or manual review

Decision-maker titles:

- Founder
- Owner
- President
- COO
- VP Operations
- Operations Manager
- Accounting Manager
- AP Manager
- Controller

Secondary contacts only when a primary decision-maker is unavailable:

- Brokerage Operations Specialist
- Billing Specialist
- Carrier Payables Specialist
- Settlements Specialist
- Load Entry Specialist
- Operations Coordinator

## Locked Pain

The prospect likely spends staff time reconciling carrier invoices against rate confirmations and completed-load documents by hand.

Common documents:

- Rate confirmation already stored in the TMS
- Carrier invoice or freight bill
- Bill of lading
- Proof of delivery
- Lumper receipt
- Accessorial backup
- Detention, layover, TONU, or re-delivery paperwork

The core gap is not generic document entry. The rate con usually already exists in the TMS, while the carrier invoice, POD, lumper receipt, and accessorial backup arrive through email, fax, carrier portal, or a shared inbox. The back-office operator toggles between the TMS, inbox, carrier portal, and document folders to decide whether the carrier should be paid.

The ROI story is margin recovery. A meaningful share of carrier invoices include a line item that does not match the rate con, often because carrier billing systems auto-apply template accessorials. Catching those mismatches before payment is more valuable than simply saving typing time.

## Public Pain Signals

Signal A: public operating workflow that suggests carrier-payables or document intake friction.

Examples:

- Instructions to email invoices, PODs, billing paperwork, claims, or accounting questions
- Carrier packet, onboarding, payment, or billing pages that route documents through email/fax
- Website language showing a carrier network, truckload brokerage, expedited, refrigerated, flatbed, drayage, or spot-market brokerage operation
- Public mention of TMS usage, load tracking, carrier portal, billing portal, or document upload
- Lean team with high-service brokerage language and no obvious automated carrier-payables process

Signal B: hiring for brokerage operations, billing, settlements, or carrier-payables work.

Relevant titles:

- Brokerage Operations Specialist
- Freight Broker Assistant
- Carrier Payables Specialist
- Billing Specialist
- Settlements Specialist
- Accounting/AP Specialist
- Load Entry Specialist
- Logistics Coordinator
- Operations Coordinator
- Track and Trace Coordinator
- Claims or Compliance Coordinator

## Offer

Done-for-you carrier invoice reconciliation that compares incoming carrier documents against the rate con/load data already in the TMS and flags exceptions before payment.

Keep the offer small and concrete:

- Pull carrier invoices, PODs, and lumper receipts from inboxes, fax folders, portals, or uploads
- Match carrier invoice line items against the rate confirmation
- Flag accessorials, totals, duplicate invoices, missing PODs, and mismatched load numbers
- Create a reviewed exception queue for ops/AP
- Start with one inbox, one TMS export, one branch, or one lane
- Work with the current TMS instead of replacing it

## Fit Rules

Qualify when:

- The company matches small freight brokerage.
- Headcount appears roughly 5-20 staff, or 10-40 when the operation is still owner/operator led.
- There is at least one real Signal A or Signal B with evidence URL.
- A plausible operations decision-maker can be identified.
- The evidence supports carrier invoice, POD, billing, settlements, load-entry, AP, or compliance work.

Disqualify when:

- The company is a carrier-only fleet, warehouse-only provider, forwarding/customs-only operator without truckload brokerage fit, marketplace, software vendor, or enterprise 3PL outside the target size.
- No real carrier-payables, billing, settlements, POD, invoice, or brokerage-ops signal can be found.
- The only evidence is generic logistics copy without a manual workflow or hiring signal.
- The company appears too large, too small, inactive, or outside the operating region being researched.

## Evidence Standard

Every pain claim must cite an evidence URL.

Every hook must be grounded in Signal A or Signal B.

Do not infer invoice leakage from industry alone. If evidence is weak, set `State` to `NEEDS_REVIEW` or `DISQUALIFIED`.
