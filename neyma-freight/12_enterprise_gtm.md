# Enterprise GTM System

## Purpose

Run Neyma Freight like a small enterprise GTM team, not a lead scraper.

The motion is account-first hybrid ABM plus SDR:

- A-tier accounts get founder-led ABM depth.
- B-tier accounts get lighter SDR-style outreach.
- C-tier accounts are held for enrichment or disqualified.

The conversion goal is a 5-minute reconciliation audit around one completed-load carrier-payables process.

## Core Model

Start with the account, not the person.

For each account:

1. Confirm small freight brokerage fit.
2. Find a real operational signal.
3. Map the buying committee.
4. Write an account point of view.
5. Draft outreach to the best persona.
6. Track touches, replies, and learning.

## Account Tiers

### A-Tier ABM

Use `Account Tier = A` only when:

- ICP fit is strong.
- Signal A or Signal B is strong.
- At least two direct person LinkedIn URLs are mapped when possible.
- At least one credible email exists.
- `Account POV` and `Workflow Audit Angle` are complete.
- Email and LinkedIn drafts are custom to the account.

Send no more than 5-10 A-tier accounts per week.

### B-Tier SDR

Use `Account Tier = B` when:

- Fit is good.
- Evidence is real.
- One decision-maker, direct person profile, or general inbox exists.
- Outreach can be specific but does not justify full ABM depth yet.

B-tier rows can be promoted after enrichment.

### C-Tier Hold

Use `Account Tier = C` when:

- Evidence is weak.
- Headcount or company type is unclear.
- Decision-maker is missing.
- Email is unavailable.
- The account needs more work before outreach.

Do not send C-tier accounts.

## Buying Committee

For A-tier accounts, map two to three people when possible:

- Founder, Owner, or President
- COO, VP Operations, Head of Operations, or Operations Manager
- Accounting Manager, AP Manager, Controller, Billing Manager, Carrier Payables, Settlements, or senior brokerage operations lead

Use:

- `Person 1 Name`
- `Person 1 Title`
- `Person 1 LinkedIn URL`
- `Person 1 Email`
- `Person 1 Confidence`
- `Person 2 Name`
- `Person 2 Title`
- `Person 2 LinkedIn URL`
- `Person 2 Email`
- `Person 2 Confidence`
- `Buying Committee`

Person LinkedIn URLs must be direct `/in/...` profiles. Company pages belong in `LinkedIn URL`.

## Account POV

Write one account-level thesis before drafting.

Example:

`They appear to run a lean truckload brokerage and route carrier/billing documents through email, so the likely workflow is carrier invoice/POD intake -> rate-con comparison -> exception review before payment.`

The POV must be grounded in the evidence URL. Do not make claims about volume, cost, or pain intensity unless the source supports it.

## Workflow Audit Angle

The `Workflow Audit Angle` field names the exact reconciliation process to inspect.

Good examples:

- `Carrier invoice -> rate-con match check`
- `POD and invoice intake -> payment-ready exception queue`
- `Lumper/accessorial receipt -> rate-con variance flag`
- `Shared billing inbox -> reviewed carrier-payables queue`

The CTA should ask for a 5-minute reconciliation audit, not a demo.

## Sequence

Use `Sequence Step`:

- `Not started`
- `Email 1`
- `LinkedIn touch`
- `Follow-up 1`
- `Follow-up 2`
- `Breakup`
- `Complete`

Use:

- `Last Touch Date`
- `Next Touch Date`
- `Reply Type`
- `Learning Notes`

Follow-up timing:

- Follow-up 1: 3-5 business days after Email 1.
- Follow-up 2: 7-10 business days after Follow-up 1.
- Breakup: only when appropriate.

## Weekly Operating Cadence

Each week:

1. Add 20-30 candidate accounts.
2. Fully enrich 10-15 accounts.
3. Promote only the best rows to A-tier.
4. Send or manually touch 5-10 A-tier accounts.
5. Review replies, bounces, objections, and positive responses.
6. Promote winning patterns into `Learning Notes` and the golden set.

Do not let available emails determine who gets contacted. The best accounts go first.
